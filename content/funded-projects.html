Research and Application Projects

Research projects and applied collaborations in which I have been involved, with my role and selected related publications.

AI4RedRiver · 2023–2025

Role: scientific participation. Led by the University of Brescia in collaboration with Thuyloi University, this Climate Change AI project (IG-2023-174) investigates AI for dam simulation and control in the Red River delta, considering water demand and energy production over a long horizon. My contribution concerns automated planning technologies. Project website.

RIPER · 2022–2025

Role: scientific participation. Resilient AI-Based Self-Programming and Strategic Reasoning is a PRIN project led by Sapienza University of Rome, with the University of Brescia as a partner. It studies resilience at the intersection of automated planning and formal methods.

Related results: Planning for Temporally Extended Goals in Pure-Past Linear Temporal Logic (ICAPS 2023) and FOND Planning for Pure-Past Linear Temporal Logic Goals (ECAI 2023).

AIPLAN4EU · 2021–2023

Role: co-leader of WP4 on planning engines and leader of Task 4.3 on numeric planning. Funded by the European Commission under H2020 grant agreement 101016442, the project made planning systems and formalisms more accessible through a common platform and API. AIPLAN4EU · Github · Demo at ICAPS-22.

Project output: Unified Planning: Modeling, Manipulating and Solving AI Planning Problems in Python (SoftwareX 2025).

Urban traffic plan validation and verification · January–March 2021

Role: principal investigator. A contracted collaboration with the University of Huddersfield on the validation and verification of urban traffic control plans through AI and automated planning.

Related publication: Effective Grounding for Hybrid Planning Problems Represented in PDDL+ (The Knowledge Engineering Review 2021).

MOSORE · 2020–2023

Role: university lead for Tasks 2.4 and 2.6 and department lead for Task 2.7. Infrastructures and Services for Sustainable and Resilient Mobility was led by the University of Brescia and funded by Regione Lombardia. I led activities on an autonomous drone architecture for monitoring and medical-material transport, including its planning and scheduling capabilities. Project information.

AWARD · 2018

Role: scientific participation. This R&D project investigated warehouse automation. Our contribution was the design of planning and scheduling algorithms for fleets of automated guided vehicles, using SMT and simulated annealing to optimise warehouse operations.

MAIS · 2017–2019

Role: scientific participation at Fondazione Bruno Kessler. Mechanical-Automation Integration System was an R&D project led by Phox for the automation of a galvanic plant. Our work addressed planning, scheduling and execution for interacting hoists, using an action-based temporal model with metric trajectory constraints to improve production. Project information.

Research motivated by this project: Temporal Planning with Temporal Metric Trajectory Constraints (AAAI 2019).

ARC: Robust AI Planning for Hybrid Systems · 2015–2017

Role: research fellow at the Australian National University, supported by the Australian Research Council. The project investigated models and methods for robust planning in hybrid systems and contributed to the development of ENHSP and SPRINGROLL. Project information.

Related publications: Interval-Based Relaxation for General Numeric Planning (ECAI 2016); Heuristics for Numeric Planning via Subgoaling (IJCAI 2016); Numeric Planning with Disjunctive Global Constraints via SMT (ICAPS 2016).

COST-STSM · 2015

Role: scientific participation in COST-STSM-TU1102-21500. The collaboration investigated learning techniques for macro-actions in automated traffic management using planning.

Related publication: Towards a Reformulation Based Approach for Efficient Numeric Planning: Numeric Outer Entanglements (SoCS 2015).

SMAT-F2 · 2013–2015

Role: scientific participation at the University of Turin. This regional project investigated environmental monitoring using UAVs. I contributed to a tactical planner based on the PDDL formalism for controlling UAV missions.

A methodological spin-off on representing temporal constraints through numeric planning was presented in A Numeric PDDL Based Approach for Temporally Constrained Journey Problems (ICTAI 2014).

STEPS · 2009–2012

Role: scientific participation at the University of Turin. Sistemi e Tecnologie per l’EsPlorazione Spaziale investigated technologies for future space missions, in a collaboration led by Thales Alenia Space. My work addressed autonomous rover plan execution and human–robot interaction, including an execution environment based on the PLEXIL formalism and sliding autonomy.

Project publications: STEPS: Predictive Control System Results on 1st Working Prototype (ICES 2010); Involving the Human User in the Control Architecture of an Autonomous Agent (IFIP AI 2010); Communication Scheduling and Plan Revision for Planetary Rovers (i-SAIRAS 2010); Intelligent Supervision for Robust Execution (AIxIA 2011).

Scientific Coordination and Collaborations

RCRA (since 2025): coordinator of the Italian Association for Artificial Intelligence (AIxIA) special interest group on knowledge representation and automated reasoning. Activities concern representation formalisms, reasoning algorithms and connections between approaches.

OVERLAY (since 2020): scientific member of the research group on formal verification, logic, automata and synthesis, connecting formal methods and artificial intelligence.

Related collaborative work: Decidability and Complexity of Action-Based Temporal Planning over Dense Time (AAAI 2020); the extended journal study (Artificial Intelligence 2022); On the Expressive Power of Intermediate and Conditional Effects in Temporal Planning (KR 2022).
